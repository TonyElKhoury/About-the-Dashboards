# About-the-Dashboards
I’ve created videos to give you a closer look at the Power BI dashboards I’ve built. I haven’t included the actual dashboards; the videos show how they work and what kind of insights they can provide. 

Want to See the Dashboards? If you’re curious to explore the dashboards themselves, let me know! I’d be happy to share them with you upon request.

The Call Center Dashboard focuses on call center operations, displaying the number of calls handled, call durations, customer orders, and agent efficiency to provide a clear overview of overall call center performance.
[Call Center Dashboard](https://github.com/TonyElKhoury/Call-Center-Dashboard/raw/refs/heads/main/Call%20Center.mp4)

The Sales Dashboard focuses on sales performance. It shows how revenue, cost and profit are connected and affect each other.
[Sales Dashboard](https://github.com/TonyElKhoury/Sales-Dashboard/raw/refs/heads/main/Sales%20Dashboard.mp4)

The Banking Dashboard focuses on banking data, showing the number of deposit account holders at commercial banks and the number of customers who have taken loans.
[Banking Dashboard](https://github.com/TonyElKhoury/Banking-Dashboard/raw/refs/heads/main/Banking%20Dashboard.mp4)
