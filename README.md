# PowerBI Dashboards
I’ve created videos to give you a closer look at the Power BI dashboards I’ve built, showcasing how they work and the insights they can provide.

While AI can help build dashboards and automate parts of the process, the real value lies in knowing what to measure, which KPIs matter, and what questions the data should answer. Defining the right metrics requires human judgment, business understanding, and a clear vision of the insights needed to support better decisions.

Want to see the dashboards? If you’re curious to explore them firsthand, feel free to reach out. I’d be happy to share them with you upon request!

The Call Center Dashboard focuses on call center operations, displaying the number of calls handled, call durations, customer orders, and agent efficiency to provide a clear overview of overall call center performance.
[Call Center Dashboard](https://github.com/TonyElKhoury/Call-Center-Dashboard/raw/refs/heads/main/Call%20Center.mp4)

The Banking Dashboard focuses on banking data, showing the number of deposit account holders at commercial banks and the number of customers who have taken loans.
